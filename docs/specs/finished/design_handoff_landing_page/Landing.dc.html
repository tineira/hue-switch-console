    Install over USB

    Plug the board into your computer and open Setup in Chrome or Edge. It installs the firmware and saves your Wi-Fi. Nothing to compile.

    Pair with your Bridge

    Press the button on your Hue Bridge when the switch asks. It sends the console your rooms, zones, lights and scenes.
